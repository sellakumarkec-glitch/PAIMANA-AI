How this works. The assistant resolves the question to a cohort (ministry, sector or state) and an intent (risk, cost, schedule, land, clearances, drivers), computes the answer directly from the scored portfolio, and renders the supporting rows. Because the numbers are computed rather than generated, the assistant cannot hallucinate a figure. To add free-form phrasing on top of this grounded context, point PAIMANA_LLM_ENDPOINT at a local open-source model — see docs/LLM_ASSISTANT.md.
