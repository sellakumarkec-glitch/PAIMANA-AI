Limitations. Cost escalation is intrinsically noisier than schedule slippage — a large share of it is driven by scope changes and price shocks that are simply not knowable from a monitoring record. Treat the cost forecast as a prioritisation signal with an error band of roughly ±7 percentage points, not as a revised cost estimate. The models are also trained on a portfolio that skews towards transport and energy; sectors with few historical projects carry wider uncertainty.
